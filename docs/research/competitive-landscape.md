# Competitive Landscape

> **Status:** Research — verified as of 2026-11-13 · **Owner:** Founder · **Related:** [Business model](../product/business-model.md) · [PRD](../product/prd.md) · [Integrations](integrations.md) · [Monetization partners](monetization-partners.md) · [Platform policies](platform-policies.md)

This page covers digital guidebook, AI guest-messaging and upsell products for short-term rentals in the US and LATAM. Official pages were checked on 2026-11-13. Prices that companies do not publish are left out.

## Executive summary

- **The professional end of the market is crowded.**
  - PMSs (Guesty, Hostaway, Hospitable) and guest-experience platforms (Enso Connect, Duve, Breezeway, Operto) already combine guidebooks, AI messaging and upsells.
  - Most charge per unit or by quote, and most reach Airbnb through PMS integrations.
- **Individual hosts are underserved.** Offers either impose minimums (Enso requires 5 units), are quote-based, or assume the host already uses a PMS.
- **LATAM has no visible equivalent.** The research found no LATAM startup that combines guide, AI and upsells with visible traction. The search was not exhaustive by country.
- **Airbnb's API is closed to new entrants.** Hospitable, Guesty and Hostaway hold direct API access as Preferred+ partners. New entrants must connect through a PMS or work without integration.
- **Upsell commission benchmarks run from 0% to 10%.** Hospitable takes 7–10%, Touch Stay 5% on its free plan, and Hostfully 1%. Chekin charges no monthly fee for its upselling module.
- **The main threats** are Airbnb's native Services and Experiences (live since 2025-05-13 and enabled by default) and large PMSs bundling AI and upsells into their plans.
- **Incumbents are well funded.** Hostaway raised USD 365M (December 2024), Duve a USD 60M Series B (December 2025), and Guesty a reported USD 130M (2024).

**Airbnb Services in practice.** Services is enabled by default on hosts' listings and is **not available in Brazil or Puerto Rico** ([Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.co.uk/resources/hosting-homes/a/now-you-can-airbnb-more-than-an-airbnb-730)).

**Integration constraint.** Airbnb is not accepting new API access requests; access is by invitation only ([Hospitable](https://hospitable.com/airbnb-partnerships); [BnBCalc](https://www.bnbcalc.com/help/articles/does-airbnb-have-an-api), October 2026, third-party source). A new entrant has two options:

- connect through a PMS (Hospitable, Hostaway, Guesty, Stays, Lodgify);
- run without integration, using a link, a QR code or iCal.

See [integrations.md](integrations.md).

## LATAM and Spanish-language players

- **Stays (Brazil)** is the reference PMS in Brazil. It describes itself as number one in LATAM, with 17,000+ properties (self-reported; see [integrations.md](integrations.md)).
- **Hospeda (Brazil)** offers a digital guide with iCal.
- **Lodgify, Avantio and Chekin** are Spanish (Spain) companies.
- **No LATAM startup was found** that combines guide, AI and upsells with visible traction. The absence is itself a finding, but the search was not exhaustive by country.
- **Most Mexican Airbnb hosts are occasional hosts** ([Airbnb](https://news.airbnb.com/ea/cada-vez-mas-mexicanos-aprovechan-su-hogar-para-ser-anfitriones-ocasionales-en-airbnb/)). Per-unit, PMS-dependent tools do not target that kind of host.

## Gaps and differentiation opportunities

1. **Individual hosts, especially in LATAM.**
   - Current offers impose minimums (Enso: 5 units), are quote-based (Duve, Operto, Akia, Breezeway, Guesty Pro), or assume a PMS.
   - That leaves room for a free guide that works without a PMS, with optional integration later through Hospitable or Stays.
   - Hospitable offers a free plan and holds direct Airbnb API access. Whether its partner API is reachable on that plan is disputed (see Unverified).
2. **Language.**
   - Almost all products are built in English first, which leaves room for a Spanish-first guide and agent.
   - The research notes that in LATAM the dominant guest channel is WhatsApp. Guidesk has rejected WhatsApp, partly because of Airbnb's limits on requesting guest contact data (see [platform-policies.md](platform-policies.md)). The in-guide chat therefore has to carry this need.
3. **Local payments.** Incumbents charge through Stripe in the US and EU. Almost none offer Pix, OXXO, PSE, SINPE Móvil or installments (see [monetization-partners.md](monetization-partners.md)).
4. **Local inventory.**
   - Viator and GetYourGuide carry thin inventory in secondary LATAM destinations, while Civitatis is strong in Spanish.
   - A marketplace built on the host's own providers (chef, transfers, cleaning) is less common outside the US. That supports Guidesk's owner add-ons.
5. **Compliance by design.** Airbnb's 2026 policy permits optional, disclosed recommendations. Enso has already published how it complies (see [platform-policies.md](platform-policies.md)), so compliance is table stakes rather than a moat.

## Threats

- **Airbnb Services and Experiences.**
  - Airbnb has sold Services and Experiences inside its app since 2025-05-13.
  - Services is enabled by default on listings and costs hosts nothing.
  - This competes directly with Guidesk's tours and services commissions everywhere except Brazil and Puerto Rico, where Services is not available.
- **PMS bundling.**
  - Guesty includes Guest Upsells and AI Hub in its Ultimate package.
  - Hostaway launched an AI suite in March 2025 and offers native upsells.
  - Hospitable bundles AI, a guest portal and upsells at a 7–10% commission.
  - The same PMSs are Guidesk's planned integration partners, which creates a possible conflict of interest in partner approvals.
- **Capital asymmetry.** Hostaway (USD 365M) and Duve (USD 85M total) can outspend a new entrant on product and distribution.
- **Integration gatekeeping.** Airbnb's closed API gives incumbents with direct access a structural advantage in reaching the native message thread.

## Implications for Guidesk

- **Positioning.** Guidesk should be the free, PMS-optional guidebook for individual hosts in LATAM and the US, plus a path into professional managers once PMS integrations land. The freemium guide is the wedge against per-unit and quote-based incumbents.
- **Take rate.** Commission on owner add-ons should be tested against the observed 1–10% range. For tours and experiences, the rate is set by each partner program (see [business model](../product/business-model.md)).
- **Differentiation should rest on:**
  - Spanish-first content and agent;
  - local payment methods;
  - a marketplace for the host's own providers;
  - working without a PMS.
- **Airbnb Services.** Guidesk should monitor Airbnb Services' rollout by country, since it directly overlaps with Guidesk's service commissions.
- **PMS partner dependency.** Partner relationships should be treated as both a channel and a competitive risk. That is one more reason to keep the guide valuable without any integration.

## Unverified / open items

- **Funding:** several rounds are unverified (Hospitable, Hostfully, HostBuddy, Akia, YourWelcome, GuestJoy, Operto, Stays, Touch Stay; Enso after 2021; Guesty's total).
- **Pricing:** third-party prices for Hospitable, HostBuddy, YourWelcome and Chekin contradict one another.
- **Akia:** the "from USD 43/month" price comes from a single source.
- **Stays:** its "Preferred+ 2026" status is self-declared only.
- **Hospitable:** this research cites a free plan with direct Airbnb API access. The [integrations](integrations.md) research says Hospitable's partner API is only on paid plans (Host USD 29/month). Which hosts Guidesk can reach through Hospitable is unresolved.
- **LATAM search:** coverage was not exhaustive by country.
