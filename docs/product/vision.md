## Vision

Every guest gets an instant, accurate answer about their stay, in their language, wherever they
are already talking. Every host earns more from each stay and spends less time in the inbox.

## The problem

Short-term rental hosts run a hospitality business from their phones.

- **The same questions, every stay, at any hour.** Wi-Fi, check-in, parking, air conditioning,
  trash, checkout. Hosts answer them over and over, at night, often in a language they are not
  fluent in.
- **Knowledge is scattered.** It lives in listing text, PDFs, phone notes, and the host's memory.
  Guests don't read long house manuals, so hosts end up answering in chat anyway.
- **Revenue is left on the table.** Guests ask "what should we do?" or "can we check in early?".
  The host says no, improvises, or never offers anything.
- **The tools were built for someone else.** Guest-experience tools are English-first and usually
  assume a property management system (PMS). Many are sold by quote or require minimum unit
  counts (see [competitive landscape](../research/competitive-landscape.md)). That leaves
  individual hosts in Latin America (LATAM) largely unserved.

## The solution

Guidesk has three layers, and each one builds on the previous one.

1. **The Guide.** A complete, mobile-first digital guidebook for each property: check-in, access,
   Wi-Fi, appliances, house rules, local recommendations, emergencies, and checkout. Guests open
   it from a link or a QR code, with no app or account required. It is free to start, and it is
   the reason hosts adopt Guidesk.
2. **The Agent.** An AI concierge that knows the guide. It answers guests inside the rental
   platform's chat (Airbnb, Booking.com, Vrbo, through PMS integrations) and inside the guide.
   Hosts decide how autonomous it is, topic by topic. Anything sensitive escalates to a human.
3. **The Marketplace.** Optional services offered when they are relevant: host add-ons such as
   early check-in, late checkout, and transfers; tours and experiences; reservations; and later,
   insurance. Every offer is opt-in and disclosed, and no offer is ever a condition of access.

## Who it is for

| Segment | Main need | Guidesk promise |
|---|---|---|
| Individual hosts (1–5 listings, usually no PMS) | Stop answering the same questions; look professional | A great guide in minutes, an assistant that drafts replies, extra income. Free to start. |
| Professional managers (10+ listings, on a PMS) | Reduce inbox load across many units and a team; add revenue per stay | An agent that answers in the platform chats through their PMS, team workflows, upsell revenue |
| Guests | Fast, correct answers and a smooth stay | One place for everything about the stay, with instant answers in their language |

Details are in [Personas](personas.md).

## Positioning

> For short-term rental hosts and property managers in the Americas who are overwhelmed by
> repetitive guest questions, **Guidesk** is a property guide and AI concierge that answers
> guests where they already talk and turns those conversations into extra revenue. Unlike
> PMS-bundled tools and English-first guidebooks, Guidesk works with or without a PMS, speaks
> Spanish and English natively, and is free to start.

## Differentiators

1. **Works with or without a PMS.** Every host gets the guide, the in-guide chat, and a reply
   copilot. Hosts on a supported PMS also get replies inside the platform chat.
2. **Built for the Americas.** Spanish and English from day one, a Spanish-language experience
   catalog, and payment methods suited to LATAM on the roadmap.
3. **Guide-first.** The guide is valuable on its own, so adoption doesn't depend on trusting AI
   from the first day.
4. **Compliant by design.** Guidesk follows Airbnb's 2025–2026 policies: the guide is optional,
   benefits are disclosed, and no guest contact data is collected. This reassures hosts who are
   afraid of being delisted. See [platform policies](../research/platform-policies.md).
5. **A trust ladder.** Autonomy grows per topic, from draft to auto to full auto, as the agent
   proves itself.

## What Guidesk is not

- **Not a PMS or channel manager.** Guidesk does not manage pricing, OTA calendars, or bookings.
- **Not a WhatsApp or SMS bot.** Guests talk to Guidesk in the platform chats or in the guide.
  This was a product decision on 2026-11-13.
- **Not an off-platform booking tool.** Guidesk never moves a reservation or its payment off the
  platform where it was made.

## North Star metric

**Guest questions resolved without host intervention, per active property per month.**

Supporting metrics:

- Host time saved
- Services revenue per stay
- Free-to-paid conversion

Definitions and targets are in the [PRD](prd.md#8-success-metrics).

## Strategic bets

1. A free, excellent guide is a cheaper acquisition channel than outbound sales.
2. PMS partner programs, starting with Hospitable and Hostaway, will approve Guidesk within the
   MVP window and give it the in-platform chat.
3. Guests will buy optional services when the offer arrives at the right moment and in their
   language.
4. Hosts will grant more autonomy once the agent has proven itself.

Each bet has validation criteria in the [roadmap](roadmap.md) and an owner in the
[open questions](../open-questions.md) log.
